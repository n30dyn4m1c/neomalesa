Collection

Papua New Guinea read through its own data — climate records, budgets, politics, and public statistics rendered so the pattern in them can be seen. Newest work is added here as it is published.

  • Pacific Dataviz Challenge · 2026 The Ocean Knows First Winner · Grand Pacific Prize Interactive scrollytelling on El Niño and drought in Papua New Guinea, built on the Pacific Community climate record. Nine chapters trace the signal from the equatorial Pacific to dry tanks, stranded barges, highland frost, and the floods that follow — closing on a preparation calendar for the event now developing. → View the 2026 challenge edition →
  • Static Graphic · 2026 All of PNG’s Prime Ministers Were Born Before Independence A timeline of the eight people who have served as Prime Minister, from birth through each term in office. None was born after 1975. They averaged 43 on first appointment, against a national median age of 24, and no one under 41 has taken the job since 1985. →
  • Static Graphic · 2026 Papua New Guinea Shares the Lowest-Ranked Passport in the Pacific A map and two charts of where a Papua New Guinea ordinary passport can go without a visa. Holders need one before travel to 122 of 193 sovereign states, tying with Naoero for the lowest score in the Pacific. Australia, 850 km away, requires a visa; Peru, 14,600 km away, does not. →
  • Data Story · 2026 Told to Quit. Almost No One Does. Scrollytelling on 27 public calls, made between 2019 and 2026, for a Papua New Guinea politician, public servant, or state company head to resign or step aside — and what followed each one. One resigned; four stepped aside for a while; 16 of the 27 calls changed nothing. →
