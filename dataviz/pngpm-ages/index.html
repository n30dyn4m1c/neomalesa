All of PNG’s Prime Ministers Were Born Before Independence

Eight people have served as Prime Minister since Independence on 16 September 1975. None was born after that date. The three charts below set out their age at first appointment, that age against the median age of the population, and the 12 terms in office from 1975 to 2026.

Sources

  1. Struggle, Reform, Boom and Bust: An Economic History of Papua New Guinea since Independence, ANU Press, Annex A. Prime Ministers and terms in office, 1975 onwards.
  2. World Population Prospects 2024, United Nations. Median age of the population: 16 in 1975, 23 in 2026 (projected).
  3. Prime Minister of Papua New Guinea, Wikipedia, with the linked biography of each Prime Minister. Dates of birth.
  4. PNG Government Gazette. Appointment of Sir Mekere Morauta, 1999.

Ages are calculated from exact dates of birth and appointment; averages are rounded. Acting Prime Ministers (John Giheno, 1997; Sam Abal, 2010–11) are excluded. The median age covers the whole population, including children. Data as of November 2026.
