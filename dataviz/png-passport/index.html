Papua New Guinea Shares the Lowest-Ranked Passport in the Pacific

Published 9 November 2026

Papua New Guinea ordinary passport holders must get a visa before travel to 122 of 193 sovereign states. The passport ties with Naoero for the lowest score in the Pacific — 83 destinations without a prior visa. Australia, 850 km from Port Moresby, requires a visa; Peru, 14,600 km away, grants visa-free entry for up to 90 days.
