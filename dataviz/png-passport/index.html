Papua New Guinea Shares the Lowest-Ranked Passport in the Pacific

Holders of a Papua New Guinea ordinary passport must arrange a visa before travel to 122 of 193 sovereign states. On the Henley Passport Index of 9 October 2026, the passport opens 83 destinations without a prior visa: level with Naoero, and the lowest of the 14 Pacific passports. Australia, 850 km from Port Moresby, requires a visa; Peru, 14,600 km away, admits Papua New Guineans visa-free for up to 90 days.

Reading the Map

  • Visa-free · 35 states. Entry on the passport alone, for stays set by each state.
  • Visa on arrival · 31 states. A visa issued at the port of entry; some of these states also offer it online.
  • Travel authorisation · five states: Canada, Israel, Kenya, Seychelles and the United Kingdom. No visa, but an online approval before departure, checked by the airline at boarding.
  • eVisa · 33 states, including Australia. A full visa application, made online before travel.
  • Visa required · 89 states, including New Zealand, the United States, China, Japan and every Schengen state. An application through an embassy or consulate.

By region, the share of states open visa-free or on arrival runs from 85% in the Pacific to 54% in the Americas, 41% in Africa, 30% in Asia and none in Europe. The Henley score counts visa-free, visa-on-arrival and travel-authorisation access across 227 destinations, territories included, so it differs from the state counts on the map.
